# Stories

Each captain plays their own campaign, set in its own year. The six stories share one world, the Reach, and
some characters cross between them; others never meet. This file is the story bible: what is canon, who
knows what and when, and what each story sets up or pays off. The playable text lives in
`game/ExchangeTides/datafiles/data/campaign.json` (Spanish in `data/lang/es.json`).

Method: premise and stakes first, then a five- or seven-beat structure mapped onto chapters, a voice for
every speaking character, and a shared timeline so a character in two stories never contradicts themselves
(after the *premise-workshop*, *plot-structure*, *character-management* and *series-continuity* skills of
[story-skills](https://gh.ali360.cloudns.ch/danjdewhurst/story-skills)). Dialogue was checked against
[avoid-ai-writing](https://gh.ali360.cloudns.ch/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing): no em dashes, no stacked
triplets, no "not X but Y" mirroring, no significance inflation.

## Canon facts (do not contradict)
- Silas Grimmer: Admiralty captain in 1694 (signed spoiled powder aboard the *Resolute*), commodore
  from 1694, turns raider-lord, burns the *Lark* in 1701, defeated by Vega in 1702. He moves his anchorage
  every season; only his chart-maker knows it (this is why Moreno cannot give it to Hale, and why Vega needs
  the cartographer in 1702).
- Ada Hale: Lieutenant in 1701 and 1702, Commander by 1708. Reads her letters. Owes Vega a favour.
- Hester Quill: clerk in the Ironreef mill office in 1694 (copies the ledger page for Wren); kept the
  books of Ironreef and Tidewell for eleven years; captain from 1705. Wren recognises her in 1705.
- Tobias Wren: discharged in 1694, cleared the same year, keeps the *Hammerhead*'s manifest; casts his
  own powder outside the Compact by 1705.
- Dimas Crowe: founds "Crowe & Daughters" (no daughters) by 1699; escapes the forgery charge; in 1702
  confesses Grimmer bankrolled his price-fixing.
- Nell Varga: Blackwater Cay smuggler, Calder's teacher and, from 1699, partner. In 1702 her "partner
  in {p8}" who holds the signal book is Calder, unnamed.
- Marisol Vega: heir of a ruined house in 1701; captain in 1702; head of House Vega again by 1708.
- **Governors** keep their names in every year. Magnus Thorne (Ironreef) and Liesl Vann (Tidewell) are the
  Compact's governors in 1705; in other stories they are ordinary governors.
- Each captain's closed ports come from their backstory, and their own story explains or honours it: Wren
  (Driftmoor, Isla Brasa: the *Resolute*'s grieving families), Calder (Saltmere: the old seal charges),
  Moreno (Kestrel Bay, San Velo: the price on his head), Quill (Ironreef, Tidewell: her old employers),
  Bale (Lanterna: the *Morning Star*), Vega (Kestrel Bay: her family's debts).

## The stories
Every story has 21 chapters in three acts of seven, roughly 45-50 objectives and 30-40 scenes. Each act ends
on a turn: a boss ship, a confession or a ruling.

### Bad Powder (Wren, 1694)
- Logline: a discharged master gunner proves the powder that killed his crew was rotten, and learns who
  signed it aboard.
- Acts: The Verdict (the casks; Nan Strand; Kip Wade; Quill copies the page; Grimmer's bribe) → The Carrier
  (harbour books; raiders' powder; the decoy *Tallow*; Dorn, the board's leak; the *Hammerhead*) → Coat or
  No Coat (witnesses; Grimmer's frame-up; Thorne's pensions; Quill's affidavit; the *Black Gull*; the board).
- Pays forward: Grimmer promoted (1702); Quill and Thorne (1705).

### The Second Seal (Calder, 1699)
- Logline: a wanted seal-forger finds her own mark on raiders' musket crates and has to catch the man who
  copied it.
- Acts: Wanted Again (the notice; Varga; the engraver Fenn) → The Second Seal (Crowe's silver; Brack collects
  it; Lisle and the die; Brack's brig *Whelp*) → Clean Hands (the hearing; Varga's bail; the *Gilded Purse*;
  partnership).
- Pays forward: Crowe walks free (1702); Brack, two years before she commands the *Hound* (1701).

### Turncoat (Moreno, 1701)
- Logline: Grimmer's sailing master deserts after watching him burn a ship, and buys his crew's lives with
  the only proof that can earn a pardon.
- Acts: Running (the debt; the cotton cover; Pia Sorel of the *Lark*; Vega) → The Pardon (Hale; the log;
  the *Jackal* takes Brandt; Pia's statement) → The Hound (the bounty; the chart-maker's sister; Vega's new
  ship; the *Hound*; pardon; shares).
- Pays forward: Vega (1702) is armed by Moreno, knows the chart-maker's sister will come to her.

### The Grimmer Debt (Vega, 1702)
The original seven chapters, unchanged, with fourteen new ones between them: House Vega's warehouse and
creditors, the three lost family ships, Crowe's ledger read at a bank (it names Harrow & Sons, Bale's 1708
villain), Moreno's tip, the cartographer's account, Grimmer's letter, Moreno's schooner in the line, the
supply brig *Provender*, Varga's shore battery, Hale's squadron, and House Vega reopened.

### The Compact (Quill, 1705)
- Logline: the clerk who kept two governors' books breaks their monopoly on cannon and water with a spring,
  a gunner and the ledgers she copied.
- Acts: A Clerk at Sea (the spring; casks; Wren) → Teeth (Thorne's letter; Vann's offer; the *Contract*;
  the payroll) → The Books Read Aloud (the *Anvil*; the clerks; Vann turns; the reading; the Clerks' Guild).

### The False Light (Bale, 1708)
- Logline: a navigator who swore never to return to Lanterna hunts the wreckers lighting false fires on
  clear nights, and learns why his first ship went down.
- Acts: Clear Weather (the swimmer; the 1688 chart; Ezra Mott; Kell's boats) → House Vega (insurance paid
  twice by Harrow & Sons; the *Wick*; Hale; the bait) → The False Light (Ezra's headlands; Hollis Kell's
  confession; the *False Light*; Harrow falls; a keeper's wages for Lanterna).

## Adding or editing a story
Stories are written in `tools/stories/<captain>.py`, every text as an (English, Spanish) pair, with side tasks
in `tools/stories/extras.py`. `python3 tools/stories/build.py` writes `campaign.json`, the story items,
characters and boss ships, and merges the Spanish into `lang/es.json`; never edit `campaign.json` by hand.
Then run `tools/selftest.sh`: it plays every story to the end and checks no story sends a captain to a
closed port. Story ports picked in one chapter (`{p2}`) stay the same for the rest of the story. Story
characters without a painted portrait get a face from the townsfolk parts (`npc(..., role, female, seed)`).
